How to play

Three things. That's it.

“GO!”

1. Say GO

Allow the mic, say “ready”, then shout GO. The lights drop and the paragraph appears.

The sun is up. The sea is blue. A small boat goes out…

2. Read to ride

Every word you read pushes your bike forward. Read smoothly to speed up. Ten in a row fires nitro.

crabboat

3. Swipe & dodge

Orange word on a rival? Read it to swat them off the road. Blue word on traffic? Read it to swerve past.
