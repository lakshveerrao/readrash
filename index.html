Your kit

Four things to know.

Energy

Your reading fills it: 2 words right = 1 bar, up to 3. It blocks hits and bites back: full energy knocks attackers off their bike.

Nitro

Ride a NITRO lane or read 10 in a row. Your bike rockets forward.

Dictionary
